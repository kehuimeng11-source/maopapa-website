THE IDEA IN THREE STEPS

Simple from box to bin.

The cat and Westie prints use the same proposed format. Here is how the peel-and-seal design is intended to work.

    Take one from the box

  2. 02 / USE

    Open and fill

    Use the bag for everyday pet clean-up, keeping the adhesive flap clear.

  3. 03 / CLOSE

    Peel, fold, press

    Remove the strip liner, fold the flap over, and press it closed before disposal.
